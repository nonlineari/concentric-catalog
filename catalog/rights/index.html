Rights-compatible model channels

Human-rights forums can host investigative and platform models framed as privacy, expression, due process, or remedy — content a tech-neutral crime treaty will not publish as doctrine.

  • ICCPR reporting and general comments
  • Special Procedures (privacy, freedom of expression, human rights defenders)
  • Regional systems (European, Inter-American, African) for binding or persuasive norms
  • Multi-stakeholder rights consultations adjacent to UN processes

Injection here is usually written submission, side event, or amicus-style briefing — not deposit of a ratification instrument.
