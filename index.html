Concentric international back-catalog

Inject the model into the channel that can carry it

Treaties and UN forums sit in concentric rings. Some are tech-neutral criminal-justice instruments. Others — security, rights, capacity, regional — can carry a normative or operational model the Cybercrime Convention will not. This site is a back-catalog of those rings and a method for placing work into the right channel.

Standalone research surface. Not affiliated with any record label, music site, or private monitoring repository.

The thesis

Forum shopping is already how states built parallel cybercrime instruments. The same logic applies in reverse for non-state and research actors: if one ring caps what a model may say (tech-neutral offences, LEA cooperation, evidence powers), look outward — or inward — to a ring whose mandate admits doctrine, norms, capacity templates, or rights-compatible investigation design.

Concentric does not mean hierarchical legitimacy. It means adjacent mandates you can enter without waiting for the forty-ratification clock on a single crime treaty.
