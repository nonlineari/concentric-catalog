Five rings

Each ring is a mandate cluster. Move laterally when a model needs a home the inner criminal-justice ring will not publish.

1 · Criminal justice

UN Convention against Cybercrime, Ad Hoc Committee protocol track, Budapest, Malabo, Arab, CIS instruments. Offences, procedure, MLA, electronic evidence.

2 · International security

OEWG lineage → Global Mechanism on ICTs in the context of international security. Norms, CBMs, responsible state behaviour — not criminalisation.

3 · Human rights

ICCPR / privacy / expression tracks, Special Procedures, regional courts. Normative models the crime treaty will not carry as doctrine.

4 · Capacity / development

UNODC / ITU / UNDP-style capacity programmes, donor templates, training curricula — where operational models ship as assistance, not as treaty text.

5 · Regional

AU, LAS, CoE, ASEAN, and other regional instruments and bodies. Often faster entry, different politics, reusable drafting patterns.
